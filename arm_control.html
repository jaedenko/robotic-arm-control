Connect your arm to get started

  1. Plug the Arduino into this computer with a USB cable.
  2. Press Connect and choose the Arduino's serial port.
  3. Move the arm with the sliders, the keyboard, or your own arm in front of the camera.

The controls below unlock once you're connected.
